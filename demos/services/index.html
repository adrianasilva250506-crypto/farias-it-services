About us

FAQ

Good questions

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs, like screens, batteries and clean-ups, are ready the same day. If we need to order a part, it usually takes two to three working days.

Will I lose my photos and files?

Almost never. We always make a backup before a big repair if your device still turns on, and we never look at your personal files.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you pay nothing. The diagnosis is free, and we only charge when the repair works.

Do you come to my house?

Yes. We visit homes and offices in the Leuven area for a small travel fee, or for free on the Office Care plan.
